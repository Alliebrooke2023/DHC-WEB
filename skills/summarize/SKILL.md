name: summarize
description: Summarize a file, directory, or codebase into a concise structured brief.
---

Summarize what the user points you at:

- For a **file**: purpose, key functions/classes, notable dependencies, and
  anything surprising — in under 10 bullet points.
- For a **directory/codebase**: start with list_dir and glob_files to map the
  layout, read the entry points and README, then produce: what it is (1
  paragraph), architecture (component list with one line each), how to run it,
  and open questions.

Lead with the single most important takeaway. Offer to save the summary into
the second brain (note_create) so it is retrievable later.
